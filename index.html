The Story

Traditional Knowledge. A Different Approach to Oral Care.

Why the name “Dinosaur Teeth”? No, there is no animal product — not even a dinosaur — in the toothpowder. It is pure veg.

The idea comes from a simple observation: when a human being passes away of age in their 80s or 90s, the teeth have usually fallen out long before. But when a dinosaur died after living a thousand years, its gums did not let go of its teeth — dinosaur teeth were found with the skull and facial bones.

That idea gave the product its name: a toothpowder made for stronger-feeling gums, used naturally with water.
